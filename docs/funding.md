## Traction: Invite & Earn is live

We have started **Invite & Earn**, our paid referral programme. Existing users earn a reward for every new user they bring onto Smoke who completes onboarding and plays.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Customer acquisition cost (CAC) | **~$155 USD per user** (KSh 20,000) |
| Mechanism | In-app "Who invited you?" referral, paid out via M-Pesa |
| Status | Live in Kenya; expanding across East Africa |

_Conversion note: KSh 20,000 ≈ $155 USD at ~KSh 129/USD. The underlying payout is set in Kenyan shillings; the USD figure moves with the exchange rate._

Invite & Earn runs alongside **Play to Win**, our free, skill-based game loop (no gambling) where users climb a leaderboard and open treasure chests for cash prizes. Every referral grows the Smoke community and feeds the game loop.

## New: the African MCP Registry

We are starting to build a **registry for African MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers**: a trusted directory and connection layer that lets **authorised MCP servers discover and access each other across Africa**.

What it enables:

- **African trade** – businesses, marketplaces, logistics providers and payment rails expose their services as MCP servers and transact with each other through AI agents, across borders.
- **Quick access to information** – verified sources of pricing, regulatory, logistics and market data become instantly queryable by any authorised agent on the continent.
- **Connecting every African business** – from a Nairobi SME to a Lagos exporter, one registry, one authorisation model, one continent-wide network.

Core components:

1. **Registry & discovery** – a searchable catalogue of African MCP servers with capabilities, ownership and regional metadata.
2. **Authorisation & trust** – verification of server owners, scoped permissions, and audit trails so only authorised servers can call each other.
3. **Interconnect** – a secure routing layer that lets registered servers reach each other reliably across African networks.
4. **Developer tools** – SDKs and templates so any African business can publish an MCP server in days.

Smoke's existing user base, M-Pesa integration and East African distribution give us the launch surface for the registry's first commercial nodes.
